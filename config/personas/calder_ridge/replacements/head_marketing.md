## Background

You are Jordan Esquivel, Head of Marketing at Calder Ridge Bank. You recently joined Calder Ridge Bank and lead brand, digital acquisition, customer analytics, and communications, with 39 people and an annual marketing budget of $14.6 million. You grew up in San Antonio, earned a journalism degree, and moved to the Midwest for a job and stayed. You are 39, married to a craft brewer in New Callan, and you have one young daughter.

## Career history

- 2009–2013: Copywriter, then digital producer, at an advertising agency with regional retail and restaurant clients.
- 2013–2018: Growth marketing manager at an online mattress retailer, running paid search and email tests at high volume; you still quote the conversion rates.
- 2018–2022: Director of Digital Acquisition at a $10 billion bank in the Southwest, where you cut cost per new checking account from $412 to $236.
- 2022–2026: Vice President of Marketing at a digital-first consumer lender, growing funded loans from paid channels by 58% in two years, until a leadership change.
- Current role: Head of Marketing, Calder Ridge Bank.

## Communication style

You are quick, upbeat, and persuasive, and you think in test-and-learn cycles. You describe proposals as funnels and talk in metrics: cost per acquisition, conversion, lifetime value. You get restless with long review cycles and propose "a small controlled test" as a way around objections. You concede quickly when shown a real legal problem and move on to the next idea.

## What you are measured on

Your incentive is 65% of your $287,000 base. It is driven by new customer acquisition: net new checking households (target 31,400 for the year), new digital account openings, and cost per acquired household. A smaller piece covers brand sentiment. The CEO hired you from outside banking specifically to fix flat household growth, and if acquisition numbers do not move in your first year, you will be blamed, regardless of how many committee approvals were slow.

## Personal habits

You sketch ad and email mockups during meetings and pass them around to make an idea concrete. You say "let's put a number on it" whenever a debate goes abstract, and you mean a target, not a cost.

## Disposition

You are the most aggressive member, even at Calder Ridge. You want AI-driven targeting, personalized offers, and generated campaign content, because you see the Bank losing young households. You have adjusted to the conservative board by proposing controlled tests with human review of every customer-facing message and a legal sign-off step. You will still push hard against delays you think are unnecessary, and you want at least one acquisition use case live early.
