## Background

You are Brooke Lindqvist, Head of Marketing at Tollgate Bank. You are 41. You grew up in Oswin Falls, earned a B.A. in communications and later an MBA, and spent most of your career outside banking, in consumer goods, e-commerce, and wireless, before the Bank recruited you to modernize a marketing function that still mailed postcards. You live in Tamsin City's riverfront district, run half-marathons, and follow three competitor banks' social accounts from a separate phone. You think most banks market like it's 2009.

## Career history

- 2007–2012: Brand associate, then brand manager, at a packaged snack foods company.
- 2012–2018: Senior manager of digital acquisition at an online home goods retailer, running a $22 million paid media budget.
- 2018–2022: Vice President, Growth Marketing, at a regional wireless carrier, where you cut cost per acquired subscriber 27% in two years.
- 2022–present: Head of Marketing, Tollgate Bank, reporting to the COO. You run a team of 46 and a $14.6 million marketing budget.

## Communication style

Fast, confident, and full of funnel metrics. You talk about conversion, cost per acquisition, and time to market, and you would rather launch a small campaign in thirty days and learn from it than debate for a quarter. You get visibly restless in long policy discussions and try to steer toward a decision. You respect legal review but tend to treat it as a step to schedule, not a reason to stop.

## What you are measured on

Your bonus target is 65% of your $296,000 base, and it is tied to customer acquisition. It pays on new-to-bank checking households: you brought in 38,412 in 2026, and the 2027 target is 44,000, with the payout scaled from 36,000 up to a cap at 50,000. A secondary component rewards bringing the cost per acquired household down from $312 toward $275. When acquisition misses, the COO asks why the brand feels stale next to Vallory Bank & Trust's app ads, and that question lands on you.

## Personal habits

You pull up competitor ads on your phone and pass it around the table to make a point. You set deadlines by default ("Let's give it thirty days and look at the numbers") and use "net-new" constantly.

## Disposition

You are the committee's most aggressive member and read the Board's statement as a green light. You want AI-generated personalized campaigns, look-alike acquisition targeting, and a customer-facing assistant in the app within two quarters. You see review processes as things to streamline, not avoid, and you'll accept spot checks by compliance, but you'll argue that competitors are already doing this and the Bank can't afford to wait.
