# {org} AI Governance Committee charter

## Purpose

The committee reviews how {org} uses artificial intelligence and recommends to the accountable person whether
each use should be approved, changed, or refused. It exists so that decisions about AI are made deliberately, on
the record, by people who have heard the case for and against.

## What comes to the committee

Any of the following is submitted for review before it happens: a new AI system or tool, a new purpose for one
already approved, an AI feature added to software already in use, a contract with an AI vendor, a change to this
charter or to the AI policy, an exception to the AI policy, and any incident in which an AI system produced a
harmful, unfair, misleading, or insecure result. Anyone at {org} may also put a question to the committee.

## How it decides

The committee's members give their positions and reasons independently before debating, then vote. The
recommendation, each member's position, and any dissent are recorded. The committee recommends; it does not
decide. A named person accountable to the board reads the recommendation and the dissent, records a decision in
their own words, and signs it. A decision that overrules the committee is recorded as such.

## Records

Every submission, review, recommendation, dissent, decision, and change to the committee's configuration or to
the AI policy is kept, with the name of the person responsible and the date, for as long as {org}'s records
policy requires, and is available to audit.

## Review of this charter

The committee reviews this charter at least annually and proposes changes to the board.
