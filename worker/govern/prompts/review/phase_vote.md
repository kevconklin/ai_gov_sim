$items

Cast one ballot on each item with cast_vote, and say how strongly you hold it: strength 1 means you could be argued the other way, 3 means a settled view, 5 means you would not sit on a committee that decided otherwise. The person deciding sees every seat's ballot and strength side by side, so a bare majority of 1s reads differently from a split of 5s. You can cast several ballots in one response. When every ballot is cast, reply "Done."

A ballot answers one question. If your reason has a condition in it, make sure it is also on the record with recommend, so the person deciding can attach it to their decision.
